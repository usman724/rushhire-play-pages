Delete your account

You can request permanent deletion of your Rush Hire account and associated personal data. This applies to both the Rush Hire Taxi (rider) and Rush Hire Driver apps.

Delete from the app

  1. Open the Rush Hire Taxi or Rush Hire Driver app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile / account settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

When you delete your account in the app, we permanently remove your profile, ride history, wallet records, and notifications from our systems. This cannot be undone.

Request by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address linked to your account:

info@rushhiretaxi.co.uk

Include your full name, registered email or phone number, and whether you use the rider or driver app. We process deletion requests within 30 days.

Data we delete or keep

  • Deleted: account profile, contact details, ride history, saved places, wallet/payment references held by Rush Hire, push notification tokens, and in-app messages.
  • May be retained where legally required: limited transaction or safety records needed for tax, fraud prevention, or legal obligations, kept only as long as required by law.
