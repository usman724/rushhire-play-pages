Privacy Policy

Rush Hire Taxi ("we") provides rider and driver mobile apps and a booking website. This policy explains what data we collect and why.

Data we collect

Account details (name, email, phone), precise location while booking or driving, payment/transaction information via our payment provider, device identifiers for push notifications, and crash/diagnostics data to keep the service reliable.

How we use data

To create and manage accounts, match riders with drivers, process payments, send ride updates, improve safety, and comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

We share data with service providers such as Firebase/Google (auth, database, messaging, maps), Stripe (payments), and crash reporting tools, only as needed to operate the service.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.

Your rights

You can delete your account in the app or via our account deletion page.
